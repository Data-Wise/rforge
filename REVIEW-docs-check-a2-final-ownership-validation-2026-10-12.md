## Findings and evidence

1. **Confirmed, repaired — repeated probe failure after parent loss.** A native parent launches the production guardian with `live_group_members` raising `CalledProcessError` on every call, then is killed after the workload starts. Before repair, terminal status reported `direct_child_reaped=false`, and the fixture had to kill survivors. The exception handler repeated the failed probe before both KILL and wait. It now attempts group KILL without another probe while the direct child remains unreaped, and attempts its bounded wait independently of signal failure. This remains an incomplete guardian failure, never successful replay. `test_parent_absent_repeated_probe_failure_still_kills_and_reaps` verifies KILL, direct reap, no live group members and the unchanged absolute global deadline.
2. **Confirmed, repaired — pending SIGINT at workload handle handoff.** Native `posix_spawn` queues SIGINT before returning its PID. Previously restoring the helper's signal mask raised before the guardian received the handle, leaving direct reap false. The guardian now defers delivery through its own assignment. The original mask is passed through nested deferral to the child; SIGINT is not accidentally left blocked in the workload. `test_pending_workload_sigint_retains_direct_child_handle` verifies the unreaped handle is retained, then reaped exactly once.
3. **Confirmed, repaired — interruption after pin construction.** A line-trace fault raises at the first caller line with a returned `GroupPin`. Previously cleanup had not begun and the pin remained open. Construction and caller assignment now occur inside the cleanup try and signal deferral; the original pin signal mask is preserved. `test_interrupt_after_pin_constructor_enters_cleanup` verifies closure and reap, including a still-live parent.
4. **Unconfirmed identity/proof concern; retained as a G08 gap.** Revision 7 requires surviving-guardian cleanup after parent loss and forbids stale PID killing; it does not establish liveness as an identity capability. Existing tests cover unreaped pin ownership, workload reap, guardian crash, corrupt checkpoint PID and parent loss. The guardian does not issue destructive exception signals after direct-child reap, and its unreaped child remains in the owned group before those signals. No parent-loss-plus-independent-pin-death PID-wrap/reuse bypass was reproduced, nor does an added `kill(pid, 0)` prove ownership. No such check or stale PID cleanup was added. Independent repair review and actual Ubuntu/macOS evidence remain blocking; this finding is not represented as disproved.
5. **NUL example rejected at the actual boundary; broader claim unconfirmed.** `test_nul_command_fails_before_bootstrap_without_success` already passes before repair: `posix_spawn` rejects NUL before bootstrap; guardian returns `guardian-failure`, no launched workload PID, pin reaped, no bootstrap error file. The production caller (`lib/_docexamples.py:233`) constructs a nonempty Rscript command and integer deadline. No reachable non-OSError bootstrap failure was reproduced under that contract. Catching every bootstrap exception solely on this example would not repair a demonstrated defect; bootstrap remains unchanged.
